# How to get sanshoku

Sanshoku (Mixed Triple Chow) is an elusive yaku. Even when
we make our hand ready for sanshoku, we may lose it at the very last
minute. For example, suppose we manage to get the hand ready for
sanshoku of 345, and we have a side-wait protorun <img class="singletile" src="/image/tiles/4-man.png">
<img class="singletile" src="/image/tiles/5-man.png"> as the ﬁnal
wait. We will get sanshoku only if we win the hand on 
<img class="singletile" src="/image/tiles/3-man.png"> ‌; if we win 
on <img class="singletile" src="/image/tiles/6-man.png">
, we will lose sanshoku.

On the other hand, it is possible to have a conﬁrmed sanshoku,
but doing so often entails a signiﬁcant loss in tile eﬃciency. For example, if our wait were a closed-wait protorun‌ 
<img class="singletile" src="/image/tiles/3-man.png">
<img class="singletile" src="/image/tiles/5-man.png"> instead, sanshoku
is conﬁrmed; but, a closed wait of 
<img class="singletile" src="/image/tiles/4-man.png"> is not very good. As long as
we seek to utilize side waits to maximize tile eﬃciency, sanshoku becomes diﬃcult to achieve. I will discuss the following seven methods
to capture this elusive yaku.
