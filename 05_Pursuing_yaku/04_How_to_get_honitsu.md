# How to get honitsu

How to get honitsu
Going for honitsu (Half Flush) can be a good way to achieve high
hand values. As we can combine honitsu with many other yaku,
including fanpai, toitoi, chanta (Outside Hand), ittsu, among
others,4 we can aim for mangan relatively easily. The fact that honitsu
is worth two han even when we open our hand means we can also
enhance the speed by melding without making our hand too cheap.
