# Scoring

Scorin
The scoring system in mahjong is quite complex. Getting proﬁ-
ciency in score calculation requires a lot of practice. The good news
is that scoring is automatically done once you win a hand when you
play online. Even when you play oﬄine, you can usually count on
your fellow players to help you get the correct score once you win a
hand.

However, you often need to calculate the (potential) scores of
your hand before you win the hand. This is because a lot of impor-
tant judgements you make during the game — riichi judgement, de-
fense judgement, and melding judgement, among others — depend
on the potential scores of your hand. Therefore, developing ability
to calculate the scores correctly and quickly without any help of oth-
ers is of utmost importance. I introduce some eﬃcient methods of
score calculation in this chapter before we discuss riichi, defense, and
melding judgements in the subsequent chapters.
